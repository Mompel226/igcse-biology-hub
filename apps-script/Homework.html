Set homework

Pick the parts of a lab you have taught, say who they are for and when they are due — then watch who has done them. Short, focused practice works better than a whole lab at once, so this sets stations, not labs.

Nothing here changes a mark. Completion is worked out from the labs the students have handed in, every time this page opens — so it is always current, and the spreadsheet stays one readable row per homework. A student who had already done a station before you set it counts as done; the date they last worked is shown so you can tell.
